03 / centerpiece
The centerpiece

Jevons Paradox.

When technology makes a resource more efficient to use, total consumption of that resource can rise rather than fall.

◆
Coalthe resource
⚙
Better technologymore efficient use of coal
▦
New productsfactories, transport, more use cases
↗
Coal demand ↑total consumption can rise
Efficiency lowers the effective cost of using the resource → more things become economical to build → demand expands.
WITH COAL AS THE RESOURCE: better technology expanded the market for coal-powered products.Jevons Paradox

Now do the same thing again

Plug in knowledge as the resource and LLMs as the technology.

LLMs increase the efficiency of knowledge utilisation. That makes more knowledge products economical to create, which should increase demand for knowledge.

The consequence of cheap intelligence is not less knowledge. It is a larger market for knowledge that is useful, trusted and hard to reproduce.
