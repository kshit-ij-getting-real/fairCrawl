FairFetch / Thesis deck

The market layer for scarce knowledge in the AI age.

AI is making intelligence abundant. The next bottleneck is trusted, proprietary and contextual knowledge that agents are allowed to use.

04 / FairFetch wedge

Do not build a marketplace for all information. Build the market for knowledge that is scarce, permissioned and useful at decision time.

Procedural assets

09
09 / Marketplace economics

A marketplace wins only if it removes friction from both sides.

More supply

Research owners expose unused assets and programmable rights.

Better matching

Agents find the smallest useful asset for a specific task.

More transactions

Licensing shifts from annual procurement to granular usage.

More creation

Observable demand creates incentives to produce new specialist research.

The real product is not “a database.” It is lower transaction cost for trusted knowledge.

12 / Make the thesis falsifiable

Good products are explanations of customer behavior. They should survive tests.

Problem → product fit

Test: do research agents repeatedly hit a proprietary-information gap severe enough to pay to resolve?

Hard to vary

The product should solve one specific bottleneck: rights-cleared retrieval. If “marketplace” can mean anything, the thesis is weak.

Reach

If the primitive works for research reports, it may extend to codebases, specialist datasets and other knowledge assets without changing the core transaction.

Near-term falsifier: consultants like the output but will not pay incrementally, cannot use licensed fragments inside agent workflows, or suppliers refuse granular rights.

Product framing inspired by David Deutsch's criteria for good explanations: hard to vary and broad reach; “falsifiable” used here as a product-testing discipline.
13
13 / Motion to market

Do not launch a horizontal marketplace. Manufacture liquidity in one corridor.

1. Concierge research

Serve a narrow consulting/research use case manually. Return verifiable proprietary evidence. Learn what people pay for.

2. Agent API

Turn repeated requests into structured assets, usage rights, pricing and machine-readable retrieval.

3. Marketplace

Open supply once demand patterns are legible. Standardize contracts, provenance and settlement.

Liquidity is earned query by query. The marketplace is the scaling mechanism, not the starting experience.

14
14 / What this can become

From marketplace to a capital market for research assets.

Marketplace first

Usage creates cash flows. Cash flows reveal demand. Demand creates comparable assets, prices and reputation.

Exchange later

If assets become standardized and cash flows repeatable, secondary services can emerge: ratings, forward contracts, financing, benchmarks and portfolios.

Important: this is a long-duration option, not the reason to invest in v1.
